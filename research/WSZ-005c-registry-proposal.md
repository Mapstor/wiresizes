Drafted 2026-11-11 with the data-sourcing skill, for the owner's approval. `research/data-sources.csv` is unchanged until the owner approves.

## Why the row needs a change

- Its `url`, Minnesota DLI's "Summary of Significant 2026 NEC Changes" (`…/NEC-2026-adoption-review-092525-change-summary.pdf`), returns 404 (2026-11-11). The Sources link on /guides/nec-table-310-16 and /methodology is dead (DEVLOG §5 P1).
- The row names two publishers but links one document: the dates are NFPA's, while the code changes come from DLI's summary. The data-sourcing skill (rule 5) wants each attribution to link the document that shows its claim.
