# NEC editions, state adoption, and 2023→2026 / 2020→2023 changes: primary-source findings

Retrieval date for every source below: 2026-10-19. All quotes are exact phrases returned by WebFetch's text extraction of the page or PDF. In the NFPA draft-report PDFs, strike-through and underline markup is flattened into plain text, so deleted and inserted words appear next to each other. Where that makes a value ambiguous, the item is marked UNVERIFIED.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = seen in the source cited. **UNVERIFIED** = not seen in any fetched source. **CONFLICTING** = sources disagree.
Source class: (A) government or NFPA itself; (B) standards body, IAEI, NEMA, UL, university; (C) other, used only as a pointer.

## Access notes (read first)

- **NFPA pages render with JavaScript, so WebFetch sees only metadata.** This applies to www.nfpa.org (the document page, product page, free-access page and NEC enforcement-map page) and to link.nfpa.org (NFPA LiNK, which returns "You need to enable JavaScript to run this app."). None of NFPA's own edition data or map data could be read from these pages.
- **Readable NFPA host:** docinfofiles.nfpa.org. It serves the code-development PDFs (Second Draft "PrelimSR" working drafts, First Draft meeting minutes, Public Input submittals, the NITMAM/CAM report, Standards Council decisions and TIAs). These are NFPA primary documents. The PrelimSR files are labelled working drafts ("content not final, subject to revision").
- **content.nfpa.org** (Standards Council agendas and minutes) returned ROBOTS_DISALLOWED. I did not route around it.
- **Rate-limited (HTTP 429), not re-fetched:** (1) https://www.oregon.gov/bcd/codes-stand/Documents/26oesc-table1-E.pdf, on a second read for verbatim 210.8/210.12 lines (the first read succeeded and is used below); (2) https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/70/70_A2025_NEC_P02_SD_PrelimSR.pdf, on a final read for the 120.5(E) and 120.13 text (earlier reads succeeded and are used below).
- **WebSearch budget ran out partway through.** The 200-search-per-turn limit is shared across agents. After that, only URLs already seen, or built from patterns seen on the same official host, were fetched.
- Several draft PDFs were truncated by the fetch tool. "No SR seen" therefore means only "not in the portion that could be read".

## 3. 2026 reorganization and changes (2023 → 2026)

### 3.1 Article 220 moved to new Article 120 (Chapter 1): CONFIRMED
- MN DLI NEC 2026 Adoption Review minutes, 2025-09-25 (A): https://www.dli.mn.gov/sites/default/files/pdf/NEC-2026-adoption-review-092525-minutes.pdf. Quote: "Formerly Article 220; renumbered to 120."
- "Key Updates in the 2026 NEC", Gary Flom (Intertek), IAEI Magazine, 2025-07-01 (B): https://iaeimagazine.org/columns/certification-insights/key-updates-in-the-2026-nec-that-electrical-professionals-need-to-know/. Quotes: "Article 220, which governs electrical load calculations, is being relocated to Article 120 within Chapter 1."; "While the content remains largely the same, inspectors must be aware of the new location."
- NFPA Code Talk column, Dean Austin (NFPA Senior Electrical Content Specialist), IAEI Magazine, 2025-07-01 (B): https://iaeimagazine.org/columns/nfpa-code-talk/look-at-proposed-2026-nec-changes/. Quote: "proposed relocation of the existing Article 220 to new Article 120."
- NFPA Second Draft (CMP-2) working draft, Oct 2024 (A): https://docinfofiles.nfpa.org/files/AboutTheCodes/70/70_A2025_NEC_P02_SD_PrelimSR.pdf. Every load-calculation Second Revision is numbered 120.x, for example "120.54 Electric Clothes Dryers — Dwelling Unit(s)".
- **Status: VERIFIED.**
- **Also moved:** Energy-management (EMS) content from Article 750 went to new Article 130 ("Energy management systems have been relocated from Art. 750 to Art. 130", EC&M (C) https://www.ecmweb.com/national-electrical-code/code-basics/article/55387143/global-code-wide-changes-and-overview). The 2023 220.70 EMS load provision became **120.7 "Power Control System (PCS)"** (NFPA SD SR-7785; MN summary: "The energy management system (EMS) has now been renamed Power Control Systems (PCS).").

### 3.2 Section mapping for the load-calculation items

**The renumbering is mostly the 220.x → 120.x prefix swap, with three exceptions:** the dwelling lighting load drops from 3 to 2 VA/ft²; a new 120.13 is added; and the user's 2023 references to "Table 220.12" and "Table 220.42" do not match what the 2023 edition appears to contain (see the first and fifth rows).
