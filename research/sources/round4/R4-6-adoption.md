Method:
- WebSearch: 24 of 24 used (log in section 7). NFPA host: not used.
- Every quote came through WebFetch's extraction model, asked for verbatim text in segments under 120 characters. Where it gave a paraphrase, the entry says "paraphrase".
- Only government documents count as primary for this brief. Cornell LII, Justia, state-rules.com, ICC-NTA, IAEI and city or county documents are pointers or corroboration only, and no value below rests on them alone.
- No permission or copyright notice was captured on any government page. The prompts did not ask for one; these are public records.
- Canaries: not used. These are single-line adoption facts, not table cells. The one table (Maryland's code matrix) was pulled twice, once as data and once as raw lines; the pulls matched.
